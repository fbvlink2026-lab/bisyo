📐 PORMULA

• Gasolina = (Distansya × 2) ÷ km/L

• Kabuuang Gastos = Gasolina × Presyo + ₱20 × Araw

• Kita = Kabuuang Singil − Kabuuang Gastos

• Kita/Oras = Kita ÷ Kabuuang Oras
